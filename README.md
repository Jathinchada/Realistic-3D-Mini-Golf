## 🌟 Game Overview

**REALISTIC 3D MINI GOLF** is a fully playable 3D miniature golf experience developed with Unity and C# for Game Mechanics and Design Development.

Features:
- **5 Championship Courses:**
  - **Hole 1: Peaceful Garden:** Serpentine putting track surrounded by cherry blossoms, birch trees, and flowerbeds. (Par 2)
  - **Hole 2: Curved Pond Pathway:** Sweeping curve flanked by tranquil ponds and water hazard recovery mechanics. (Par 3)
  - **Hole 3: S-Bend Nature Track:** S-shaped fairway winding through pine and oak clusters, bushes, and granite boulders. (Par 3)
  - **Hole 4: C-Shaped Pond Chicane:** Scenic C-curve hugging a landscaped pond with lily pads and reeds. (Par 3)
  - **Hole 5: Elevated Wooden Bridge:** Dramatic finale crossing a scenic pond via an elevated wooden bridge with timber trestles. (Par 3)
- **Practice Putting Green:** Dedicated warm-up putting green near the entrance with dual cups.
- **Realistic Ball & Green Physics:** Continuous Dynamic collision detection, weighted 2.5 kg physical golf ball, calibrated rolling turf resistance, and untextured smooth putting surfaces.
- **Sunken Cup Geometry:** Realistic sunken cups (0.58m diameter) with subtle funnel dynamics and celebration particle effects.
- **Audio & Nature Ambiance:** Procedural sound synthesis engine providing putter impacts, turf rolling friction, water splashes, cup sinking rattles, and background music with in-game mute toggle.
- **Full HUD & Scorecard:** Live stroke counter, par tracker, cumulative tournament scorecard (`[Tab]`), map overview (`[M]`), and hole-completion modals.

## 🎮 Controls

| Action | Input (Mouse & Keyboard) | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Aim Putter** | **Hold Left Click & Drag** | Drag away from ball to draw back putter and aim |
| **Adjust Power** | **Pull Distance** | Pull back further for stronger stroke (shown on power gauge) |
| **Strike Ball** | **Release Left Click** | Release mouse button to hit the ball toward target |
| **Rotate Aim** | **A / D** or **Left / Right** | Fine-tune your aim angle while holding shot |
| **Cancel Shot** | **Right Click** | Abort current swing without penalty |
| **Orbit Camera** | **Right Click + Drag** | Freely orbit camera around ball |
| **Zoom View** | **Mouse Scroll Wheel** | Zoom in / out smoothly |
| **Overview Map** | **M** or **O** | Toggle top-down aerial map view of the hole |
| **Scorecard** | **Tab** | Toggle cumulative 5-hole tournament scorecard |
| **Reset Ball** | **R** | Re-tee ball to start of current hole (adds 1 penalty stroke) |
| **Toggle Music** | **T** | Mute or unmute background soundtrack |
| **Pause / Resume** | **Esc** or **P** | Open pause menu with settings, restart, and quit options |
